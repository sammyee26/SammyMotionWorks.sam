About SMW

What works in this version?

Local video playback, cue editing, synchronized virtual motion preview, cue import/export, episode organization, game cue profiles, local marketplace drafts, and an illustrative token timer.

What is planned, not implemented?

Public uploads, creator verification, spendable tokens, streaming catalog, real DVD/Blu-ray playback, hardware motion rig control, and live game integration.

Privacy and compatibility

Your selected video plays locally and is not uploaded. Browser storage can be cleared by your browser, so export cue backups. Video compatibility depends on your browser and device. SMW does not bypass digital rights management or copy protection.

Physical motion safety

Only use future physical-rig integrations with validated manufacturer protocols, conservative limits, and independent emergency-stop systems. The current virtual rig is visual only.
